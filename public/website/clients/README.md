# Client logo assets

These are 21 individually regenerated client-logo reproductions, identified from the owner-provided catalog and checked against official brand sources. They replace the earlier catalog screenshot crops at the owner's request.

The WebP files have true alpha where appropriate and retain intentional brand backgrounds. The manifest records accessible names and exact production dimensions.

High-resolution PNGs, research sources, and exact ImageGen prompts are kept in docs/client-logos/ in the repository, outside the published website's public assets. Client marks remain the property of their respective owners.
